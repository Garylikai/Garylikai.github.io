## Garbage in, garbage out

“Garbage in, garbage out” is one of my favorite principles from computing and data analysis: flawed inputs and poorly framed assumptions can undermine even sophisticated methods.

I came to appreciate the principle more deeply while taking [CSE 519: Data Science](/grad/cse519/) in Fall 2021. It was the first time I sat down to think carefully about data science as a discipline rather than only as a collection of tools. Working through Python-based analysis and modeling helped me see how computer science, statistics, and domain knowledge depend on one another.
