## Teaching approach

I aim to make rigorous material approachable without flattening it. In statistics courses, that means helping students understand not only how to obtain an answer, but also why a method is appropriate, which assumptions matter, how computation supports the analysis, and how to communicate a conclusion responsibly.

## Research mentoring

In summer 2026, I served as a graduate student research mentor in the Simons Summer Research Program at Stony Brook University. I supervised Jack Lu’s project, *Independent Spatial and Temporal Evidence for Temperature-Mediated Marine Range Shifts*, and provided guidance on study design, statistical modeling, robustness checks, interpretation, scientific writing, visualization, and poster preparation.

This work belongs here as a mentoring experience rather than as one of my GitHub repositories. The final poster was authored by Jack Lu, Kai Li, and Wei Zhu.
