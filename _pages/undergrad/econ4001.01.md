## Course description

Theory of consumer behavior; theory of the firm; costs and production; factor price determination; general equilibrium.

## Textbooks and resources

### Required textbook

- *Intermediate Microeconomics: A Modern Approach* by Hal R. Varian, 9th edition, 2014, W. W. Norton & Company

### Supplementary textbooks

- *Intermediate Microeconomics with Calculus: A Modern Approach* by Hal R. Varian, 2014, W. W. Norton & Company
- *Microeconomics* by B. Douglas Bernheim and Michael Whinston, 2nd edition, 2013, McGraw Hill
- *Microeconomics: Theory and Applications with Calculus* by Jeffrey M. Perloff, 4th edition, 2016, Pearson
- *Microeconomics* by Robert Pindyck and Daniel Rubinfeld, 9th edition, 2017, Pearson
- *Microeconomic Theory: Basic Principles And Extensions* by Walter Nicholson and Christopher Snyder, 12th edition, 2017, Cengage
