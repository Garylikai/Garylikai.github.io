## Course description

Basic linear regression analysis with applications; hypothesis testing and model specification.

## Textbooks and resources

### Required textbook

- *Introduction to Econometrics* by James H. Stock and Mark W. Watson, 4th edition, 2018, Pearson

### Supplementary textbooks

- *Introductory Econometrics: A Modern Approach* by Jeffrey M. Wooldridge, 7th edition, 2019, Cengage
- *Using Econometrics: A Practical Guide* by A. H. Studenmund, 7th edition, 2016, Cengage
- *An Introduction to Modern Econometrics Using Stata* by Christopher F. Baum, 2006, Stata Press
- *Mastering 'Metrics: The Path from Cause to Effect* by Joshua D. Angrist and Jörn-Steffen Pischke, 2014, Princeton University Press
