## Course description

Financial transactions involving interest: measurement of interest, force of interest, annuities-certain, introduction to financial derivatives.

## Textbooks and resources

### Required textbooks

- *Mathematics of Investment and Credit* by Samuel A. Broverman, 7th edition, 2017, ACTEX Publications
- *Derivatives Markets* by Robert L. McDonald, 3rd edition, 2012, Pearson

### Supplementary textbook

- *A Basic Course in the Theory of Interest and Derivatives Markets: A Preparation for the Actuarial Exam FM/2* by Marcel B. Finan, 2017
